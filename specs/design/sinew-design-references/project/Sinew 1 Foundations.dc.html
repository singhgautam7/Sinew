WHY THESE VALUES
Measured the eight siblings in OKLCh: dark L 0.34–0.40, mid L 0.52–0.64, tint L 0.90–0.945 at chroma ≈0.04. Your brief's tint (#EDF4CC, L 0.952, C 0.053) was lighter and more saturated than all eight, so it read as neon next to them.

KEPT APART FROM POSTPURUSH
PostPurush sits at hue 145, Unfurl at 63–79. Sinew takes hue 121, 24° from green and 42° from amber. Mid chroma 0.135 is higher than PostPurush's 0.100, so Sinew reads yellow-green, not leaf green.

Dynamic colour: Android hands over one wallpaper seed and MullFamily.fromSeed derives the rest. Mocks use a blue wallpaper (light) and a coral wallpaper (dark).
AMOLED is a third mode for every family, Sinew and dynamic alike: surface is #000000, containers step up from black by tone only, and onSurface / onSurfaceVariant take the family's tint and mid tones instead of grey. Same formula with primaryHue swapped, so Kuber, Mull and the rest get their own AMOLED for free. Both text roles clear 10:1 on black.
Contrast: primary on surface 4.91:1 (light), 7.95:1 (dark). The light primary is stepped down from L .55 to .53 by Kuber's guardedLightness until white labels and the deepest card pass 4.5:1.
Fixed accents, harmonised
Each fixed hue is pulled toward Sinew's primary hue (half the distance, at most 15°), the same idea as Kuber's color_harmonizer. In dynamic mode they harmonise to the wallpaper hue instead.

App icon · Plate S
Same tint, dark and mid as the siblings. The plate rim is ~31/512, a little lighter than the siblings' 44/512 strokes, to leave room for the hub and hole. Two plates make the S, so it works as the letter and as gym equipment at once. Every format below uses A until you pick.
A · Plate S
96 · 48 · 24dp · themed mono light and dark · right: dark splash and mono dark
Two weight plates seen face-on, each a thick ring with a hub. The top plate is open at the lower right and the bottom plate, the same shape turned 180°, is open at the upper left. They meet at the centre, so the pair is the S. Dark is the top plate, mid the bottom, and mid laps over dark where they join, the same lap as the other family glyphs. Round ends match the siblings.
B · Plate S, cut ends
96 · 48 · 24dp · themed mono light and dark · right: dark splash and mono dark
The same two plates with square-cut ends, so each reads as a plate with one quarter taken out. It looks more machined and is a little further from the family's round-ended strokes.
