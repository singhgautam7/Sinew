About BMI
Body mass index compares weight to height. It's a quick population measure, not a verdict on one body.
FORMULA
weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²
78.4 ÷ 1.78² = 24.7
{{ b.k }}{{ b.v }}
Asian cut-offs
Overweight from 23, obese from 27.5
LIMITS
BMI doesn't account for muscle, bone or where fat sits. Strong, muscular people often read as overweight. It isn't designed for children or pregnancy. Waist-to-height ratio is a useful second view.
